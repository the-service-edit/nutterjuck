The Codmotion marked NutterJuck's entry into larger-profile native fish lures. Inspired by the proven success of the OG Bass Walker, the goal was simple: take everything that made the original a consistent fish catcher and scale it up for Australia's most iconic freshwater predator.

Built around the same principles of simplicity, balance and performance, the Codmotion keeps the effortless walking action that made the Bass Walker a staple in tackle boxes. The result is a lure with substantial surface presence that stays easy to work and genuinely enjoyable to fish.

At 110mm and 45g it offers the larger profile Murray Cod demand without becoming cumbersome or difficult to cast. The handcrafted timber body and durable aluminium bib deliver a strong, deliberate surface action — worked slowly over timber, along river banks, or across shallow feeding zones where cod wait to ambush.

Designed for anglers who back proven concepts over passing trends, the Codmotion is a natural evolution of the range. It takes the heritage of the OG Bass Walker and applies it to larger waters, larger fish, and larger opportunities.
