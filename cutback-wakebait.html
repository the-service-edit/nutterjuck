New for 2026, the Cutback Wakebait embodies the NutterJuck philosophy of simplicity and purpose-driven design. Cut away the unnecessary, focus only on what matters. Function over fashion.

It fills the gap between traditional bass-sized wakebaits and oversized specialist lures. At 150mm it presents a substantial meal to predatory fish without becoming cumbersome to cast or retrieve — and it doesn't demand heavy tackle or a specialised setup. If you fish light and versatile gear, this lure is for you.

The balanced timber body and fibre composite bib work together to create a strong, attention-grabbing wake while holding a natural swimming action. At 15g it casts and controls beautifully, letting you cover water efficiently while presenting a larger target to fish holding on structure, weed edges, and open-water feeding zones.

Trophy Australian Bass, or other mid to micro native predators — the Cutback delivers a rare combination of size, fish appeal and fishability. Stripped back to the essentials and engineered to perform. Sometimes less really is more.
