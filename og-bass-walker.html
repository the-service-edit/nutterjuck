The OG Bass Walker is where the NutterJuck story began. As the original lure in the range, it has earned a reputation as a proven fish catcher that continues to deliver season after season.

Handcrafted from timber and fitted with a durable aluminium bib, it is built to create an enticing topwater walking action that Australian Bass simply can't ignore. The balanced design lets you work the lure across the surface with almost no effort, producing the classic walking action that triggers aggressive strikes.

Rivers, creeks, impoundments — the OG Bass Walker is a trusted choice for anglers who appreciate simple, effective lure design. More than just a lure, it's the foundation of the NutterJuck range, and proof of the philosophy behind it: build quality lures that catch fish, year after year.
