The OG Bass Walker is where the NutterJuck story began. As the original lure in the range, it has earned a reputation as a proven fish catcher that continues to deliver season after season.

Handcrafted from timber and fitted with a super sonic aluminium bib, the OG Bass Walker has been designed to create an enticing topwater walking action that Australian Bass simply can't ignore. Its balanced design allows anglers to effortlessly work the lure across the surface, producing the classic action that triggers aggressive strikes.

Whether you're targeting bass in rivers, creeks or impoundments, the OG Bass Walker remains a trusted choice for anglers who appreciate simple, effective lure design. More than just a lure, it's the foundation of the NutterJuck range and a testament to the philosophy of simple done well.
